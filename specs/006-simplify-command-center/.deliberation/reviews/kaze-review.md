# Kaze review — Simplify the Command Center

## 1. What problem is this actually solving?

Nine entries in a rail is not a feature list. It is a nightly interrogation. The owner opens the Command Center tired, and the rail asks her nine questions at once: did you check caja, did you check stock, did you check ventas, costos, producción, logística, proveedores. Every tab is a small accusation that she has not looked yet. The real job is not navigation. It is relief. She wants to walk in, feel the business is held, deal with the one thing that actually moved today, and leave. Right now the surface makes her do the scanning that the system should have already done for her. The felt problem is a flat wall of equal-volume demands with no voice telling her what matters.

## 2. What is the smallest first version that proves the idea?

Three tabs. Not one, not four.

Timonel is the main column, not a tab and not a corner widget. Tab one is "Hoy." It opens with one sentence in plain language, large, alone for a beat: what happened today and the single thing that needs her. Under it, Timonel's input, wide and inviting, already focused. Tiles live below the fold as quiet confirmations, not the main event. First five seconds, the screen says one true thing, for example "Hoy entraron tres ventas. Falta cobrar una. Todo lo demas esta en orden." That is presence. She is received, not greeted.

Tab two is "Negocio": the consolidated operating surface for productos, stock, ventas, gastos. One place she goes when she wants to touch the numbers with her hands, not talk. Tab three is "Costos y producción": the deeper, slower work of costeo, producción, logística, proveedores. She visits it on purpose, rarely, not nightly.

The shape: talk to run it, look to trust it, open to go deep. The chat drives. The tabs catch.

## 3. What 3 risks would kill this if ignored?

The chat feeling like a demand. An empty input with a blinking cursor and a placeholder like "Pregunta algo" reads as homework. It must open with Timonel already having spoken, so she is answering, not starting.

The numbers feeling untrustworthy. The moment Timonel writes money by voice, she needs to see the write land on screen with weight: the row appears, settles, stays. If the write is invisible, she will stop trusting the mouth and go back to the rail. Every AI write shows its result physically.

The cut feeling like loss. Collapsing nine into three can feel like features were taken away, not simplified. Nothing is removed, everything is still reachable in two taps, and the tiles on Hoy must visibly drill into the old depth so she feels the business is still all there.

## 4. What does success look like at 90 days?

She opens Hoy, reads one sentence, says one thing to Timonel, sees it land, and closes the laptop. The other two tabs go untouched most nights, and that is the win. She stops feeling behind. The verdict is an exhale, not a tour.

## 5. What atomic tasks does this break into?

1. Define the three-tab shape and the Hoy-first information order.
2. Design the Hoy headline: the one-sentence daily truth, type scale and silence around it.
3. Design Timonel as main column: pre-spoken opening line, focused input, resting state.
4. Design the "Atención" single-thing surface under the headline.
5. Design the write-landing choreography: row enters, settles, confirms.
6. Design the Negocio consolidated tab layout (productos, stock, ventas, gastos).
7. Design the Costos y producción deep tab layout.
8. Design tile-to-depth drill-down so nothing feels removed.
9. Define color temperature and one accent for the three-tab system.
10. Mobile: stacked order, Timonel first, tabs as bottom segments.
11. Empty and loading states for Hoy and the chat.
12. Reduced-motion pass on the write-landing and tab transitions.

## 6. The one thing only my faculty would notice

Everyone will argue about tab count. The thing that decides whether this lives is the first sentence on Hoy, before any tab exists. If that line is a dashboard label, the AI is a widget and we failed. If that line speaks to her like it already did the looking, three tabs feel like calm and nine felt like noise. The sentence is the product. Build it first.
